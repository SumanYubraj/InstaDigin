How It Works

The Fleet Management Module integrates seamlessly into your operational workflow, providing real-time oversight from dispatch to delivery. It connects with IoT gateways and CAN bus systems on vehicles to collect data, which is processed on edge devices or synced to cloud dashboards for actionable insights.

  • Role: Monitors fleet performance, optimizes routes, and ensures compliance.
  • Integration: APIs enable connectivity with ERP, fuel, maintenance, and weighbridge systems.
  • Accessibility: Mobile app and cloud dashboards provide on-the-go and centralized control.
