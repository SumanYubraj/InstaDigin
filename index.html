What Is Frappe Helpdesk?

Your Entire Support Operation. One Platform.

Frappe Helpdesk is a 100% open-source ticketing platform that captures, assigns, tracks, and resolves all tickets in one place. Built on the Frappe framework, it is fully customisable and connects to other Frappe apps on the same stack.

Why Frappe Helpdesk?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Want to Know About Frappe Helpdesk

What is Frappe Helpdesk?

A 100% open-source ticketing platform built on the Frappe framework. It manages tickets, SLAs, assignments, knowledge bases, and self-service portals. Runs on the same framework as ERPNext and Frappe CRM, so records can be linked without external middleware.

How is Frappe Helpdesk different from Zendesk or Freshdesk?

Zero license fees, unlimited agents, and same-framework linkage to ERPNext and Frappe CRM. Customer, order, and deal data accessible from within the ticket through configured links. Zendesk wins on app ecosystem and omnichannel breadth. For most Indian mid-market businesses, Frappe Helpdesk delivers core functionality at a fraction of the cost.

Is Frappe Helpdesk the same as the ERPNext support module?

No. ERPNext has basic issue tracking. Frappe Helpdesk is a standalone app with modern UI, dual portals, SLA management, knowledge base, canned responses, and advanced assignment rules. Both run on the same Frappe framework.

Can it connect to ERPNext and Frappe CRM?

Yes. All three run on the Frappe framework, so tickets can be linked to Customer, Contact, Item, and Deal records without external middleware. The depth of linkage is configured during implementation based on your use case.

Does it include a self-service portal?

Yes. Customers raise tickets, track status, and browse knowledge base articles through a branded portal.

Can we migrate from Zendesk / Freshdesk / Zoho Desk?

Yes. We migrate tickets, customer records, SLAs, and knowledge base content. Workflows configured and tested before cutover.

How long does implementation take?

Core helpdesk with ticketing, SLAs, and email: 2 to 4 weeks. With knowledge base, portal, and ERP/CRM linkage: 4 to 8 weeks.

Can you add AI to our helpdesk?

Yes. AI-powered ticket creation, intelligent routing, data extraction, response suggestions, and escalation prediction on our CygnusAlpha platform.

How much does Frappe Helpdesk cost?

100% free and open-source. Zero license fees. You pay for implementation, hosting, and support. Fixed fee based on scope, plus optional AMC.

What happens after go-live?

90 days of hypercare. Then ongoing AMC for workflow tuning, SLA updates, and knowledge base maintenance.

Is it secure?

Yes. Role-based access, audit logs, encryption, and two-factor authentication. Auriga is ISO 27001 certified.
